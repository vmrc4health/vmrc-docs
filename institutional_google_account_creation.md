# Creating a Google Account with Your Institutional Email

## Intended Audience

Academic researchers, staff, or students who want to use Google services (e.g., Drive, Docs, etc.) with their institutional email (e.g., yourname@university.edu) instead of a personal Gmail account.
In the context of VMRC, you must have an institutional Google account to access VMRC data since the data is store in Google Cloud Platorm (GCP) buckets.

## Why Do This?

Using your institutional email for your Google Account:
* Keeps your research and collaborations separate from your personal Google account.
* Helps with data sharing, organization-level access, and compliance.
* Maintains a professional identity (e.g., @university.edu).

## Steps to Create a Google Account with Your Institutional Email

1. Go to the Google Account [creation page](https://accounts.google.com) (you may need to do this in Incognito mode).
1. Click “Create account”
1. Fill in the form with your first and last name
1. Enter your personal information (date of birth, etc).
1. Click "Use your existing email."
1. Enter your work email address (you'll need to confirm that the email address belongs to you).
1. Follow the prompts to enter a strong password (preferably not the same as your institution's login)
1. Google will send a verification code to your institutional email.
1. Check your inbox and enter the code to verify.
1. Complete the setup
1. You may be asked for a phone number (useful for account recovery).
1. Accept Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

***You're done!***

You now have a Google Account associated with your institutional email address.

## Notes

This procedure does NOT turn your email into a Gmail inbox. It simply lets you use Google services with your existing institutional address.

You can now sign in to Google services using your work email (e.g., yourname@university.edu) as your username.

When collaborating or sharing documents, use this account to keep work separate from personal files.

## Managing Multiple Google Accounts

If you have other Google accounts, such a personal Gmail account, you have a
few options to manage them:
* Use Google Chrome profiles or Incognito Mode to manage them separately.
* Use the account switcher by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner of any Google app.

## Accessing the VMRC Google Cloud Project

Once you've created your institutional Google account, you still need VMRC project maintainers to associate your account with the VMRC project.
This is handled manually.

Please ask your PI (who is presumably affiliated with VMRC) to contact Dr. Jacques Ravel to have your newly created account added to your PI's group in the VMRC project.
